Updates

Patch notes

0.0.7 · Fighting styles and the new Great Ape

November 4, 2026
  • Fighting styles: press Y to fight as Turtle School, Brawler, Speedster or Crane. Each has its own guard stance, four-hit combo, dash-in and finisher, and every style deals the same damage over time.
  • Flowing combat: every strike now winds up, lands and follows through, then blends into the next hit. Your fighter steps into blows, impacts freeze for a split second, and enemies stagger, fly back or get launched.
  • A brand-new Great Ape: one giant shaggy body with glowing red eyes and fangs, plus its own walk, roar, chest pound, swipe, grab-slam, stomp and mouth blast. The ground shakes when it moves. Golden form too.
  • Every face is different: eyes, brows, mouth, nose and jaw now vary between characters, with the odd scar or freckles. Reroll yours with "New face" in the character creator.
  • Solid, enterable buildings: town buildings are solid now. Walk straight into houses, cabins and dome houses, and the roof lifts away so you can see inside. Big buildings like towers, temples, the lighthouse, labs and hangars have doors: press G to step into their own interior.
  • Dash and rocket boost (Q): on the ground Q is a quick dash; while flying it's a rocket boost at more than double speed for 20 seconds, leaving a trail of Ki. Press Q again to stop.
  • A living countryside: more than 100 hamlets, farmsteads, snowy cabins, dome-hut villages, ruins and garden shrines scattered across every planet, each with its own name.
  • Camera zoom: scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in and out. The game remembers your zoom.
  • Enemy styles: famous fighters use fitting styles, so brawlers hit heavy and speedsters strike fast.

0.0.6 · Faces, combos and polish

November 4, 2026
  • Faces: real noses, ears and brow ridges, different for every race: flat noses and heavy brows for Namekians, cheek ridges for Frost Demons, ear holes for Majins.
  • Outfits fit every body: belts, wristbands, shoulder pads and collars now follow each fighter's shape.
  • More famous physiques: 30 more legends have their own build, and female fighters have their own body shape.
  • Combo moves: every hit in a combo has its own move: jab, cross, hook, uppercut, palm strike, knee, spin kick and axe kick, with a heavier finisher.
  • Technique timing: even a quick tap shows the gathering pose before the release.
  • Six reworked techniques: ki blast, energy wave, disc, wolf fang rush, instant teleport and the giant spirit sphere.
  • Balanced sound: all 258 combat sounds now play at matching volume.
  • Co-op: tested with two real games connected; friends see your techniques exactly as you do.

0.0.5 · New bodies, new techniques

November 4, 2026
  • Real body shapes: Saiyans, Half-Saiyans, Humans, Namekians, Frost Demons and Majins each have their own build, and body types change the shape, not just the size.
  • Famous fighters: the legends you meet have their own physiques, from towering brutes to tiny masters.
  • Race details: Namekians get pointed ears; Frost Demons get armoured shoulder domes and a chest plate.
  • Every technique looks different: waves, beams, discs, spirals, giant spheres, claw flurries, shields and more, in your own Ki colour.
  • Every technique sounds different: 258 new combat sounds for gathering, firing and impact.
  • Technique poses: cup your hands for the wave, raise both arms for the giant sphere, two fingers to the forehead for the spiral beam, then a real release.
  • Afterimage: leaves a see-through copy of your fighter behind.
  • Peaceful trainees: trainees on the map only fight back after you hit them.
  • Co-op: friends see your techniques coming from your own hands.

0.0.4 · The open-world update

November 3, 2026
  • Open world: every planet is one seamless map, with 15 new places to discover and a world map (N).
  • Other World: King Yemmo's Check-In Station, and King Kae's planet floating above the end of Serpent Road.
  • Movement: tap Shift to switch between walking and running or slow and fast flight. Jump while running and charge in mid-air.
  • Transformations: a form picker (T); transforming costs Ki once and forms no longer drain it.
  • Progression: travelling to the next map needs that area's level, so train up with patrols and side quests between story chapters.
  • Ultra Instinct: dodges 20 attacks automatically, then needs 30 seconds to recover.
  • Quests: click a quest for a direction arrow; group fights now bring at least 10 enemies.
  • Co-op (beta): private rooms for up to 8 players with a six-digit code.
  • And more: 10 save slots, new animations and combat sounds, many fixes.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. The whole game is free, with no ads and no purchases.
What do I need to run it?
A 64-bit computer with a graphics card that supports Vulkan (most made since 2016), 4 GB of memory and about 1 GB of space. It runs at 60 fps on a GTX 1650-class laptop. On Android, a recent 64-bit phone.
Windows says the app isn't recognized. Is it safe?
The installer isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows SmartScreen warns about it. Choose More info → Run anyway. You can check the source code on GitHub.
Do I have to download every update?
No. After you install it once, the game checks for updates on the title screen and installs them itself.
How do I play with a friend in another country?
The six-digit code finds rooms on the same Wi-Fi. Across the Internet, the easiest way is the free app Tailscale: both of you install it, you create the room, and your friend types your Tailscale address (ws://100.x.x.x:8910) in Host address, plus the code. Router port forwarding (TCP 8910) also works. When Windows asks, allow the game on the network.
Where are my saves? Can I move my fighter?
Saves stay on your device, with 10 fighter slots. Use Save codes on the title screen to copy a fighter to another device.
Is it on iPhone or iPad?
Not yet. iPhone games can only be installed through the App Store.
